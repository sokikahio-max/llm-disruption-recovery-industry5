# Results-to-manuscript map

- `governance_action_frequency.csv`: selected-action frequencies.
- `paired_statistical_tests.csv`: paired comparator tests and effect sizes used for operational comparisons.
- `benchmark_stratified_tests.csv`: benchmark-specific statistical checks.
- `weight_profile_summary.csv`, `global_weight_scenario_stability.csv`: objective-weight sensitivity.
- `threshold_summary.csv`: governance-threshold sensitivity.
- `proxy_perturbation_summary.csv`: action-specific proxy-coefficient sensitivity.
- `selected_scenarios.csv`: 12 scenarios used in the explanation study.
- `template_automated_scores.csv`: deterministic-template evaluation.
- `llm_automated_scores.csv`: 60-call LLM factual-grounding evaluation.
- `llm_consistency_summary.csv`: repeated-output consistency, latency, and cost by scenario.
- `llm_run_status.json`: overall 60-call totals and model configuration.
- `manuscript_numerical_verification.csv`: final cross-check of manuscript numerical claims.
